Instruction trace

One instruction per line, in the order they execute. Supported: ADD SUB AND OR SLT (3 registers), ADDI R1, R2, 5, LW R1, 0(R2), SW R1, 0(R2), BEQ/BNE R1, R2, T or NT (taken / not taken), NOP. Registers R0 to R31; R0 is always zero. Lines starting with ; or # are comments.
